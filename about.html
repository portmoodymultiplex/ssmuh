The Story

He walked this road before advising on it

When BC's multiplex rules landed, Dawar did not wait to see how they would play out for clients. He filed on his own property. As the first homeowner in Port Moody to move a multiplex application forward, he worked through the zoning standards, the servicing requirements, and the practical realities of the process directly with city staff, then completed specialized professional training in SSMUH development.

That firsthand experience is the foundation of SSMUH.ca: he knows where projects get stuck because he has been stuck there, and he knows what moves them forward because he has moved his own.

"I've never been a 'this sale' guy. I want to know where you want to end up, then build the road to get you there."

Dawar Zada

That philosophy shaped his career long before Bill 44. He has built a reputation for helping clients climb step by step, from a first condo to a townhouse to a detached home, and now, for some, to a multiplex. Not moving fast. Moving deliberately.

Dawar lives in the Tri-Cities with his wife, three kids, and Bear the Samoyed. Keeping family close and building something that lasts for the next generation is not a marketing angle for him; it is the reason his own multiplex application exists.
