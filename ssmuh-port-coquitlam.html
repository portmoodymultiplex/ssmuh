SSMUH in Port Coquitlam, permits and all.

The Rules Here

What Port Coquitlam allows

Properties zoned RS (residential small-scale), including RS1 through RS4, permit 3 to 6 SSMUH units depending on lot characteristics and transit proximity. You can confirm a property's zoning and OCP designation on PoCoMAP.

The step that distinguishes PoCo: new duplexes, projects with 3 or more units, and accessory dwelling units need a SSMUH Development Permit before the building permit. The upside is that council has delegated approval authority to staff for designs that comply with the zoning bylaw and OCP guidelines, so a clean, compliant application moves faster than the extra step suggests.

Budget Reality

The costs PoCo is unusually honest about

Port Coquitlam publishes more cost transparency than most municipalities, and the numbers matter:

01

Development cost charges

DCCs apply to SSMUH projects across five categories, though new secondary suites and accessory buildings are exempt. Rates change; the city's DCC page always has the current schedule.

02

Offsite servicing

Connecting new units to city infrastructure means work outside your property line, and it is charged per property. The city's own guide notes recent corner lots with lane access on two sides exceeding $100,000 in additional offsite costs. This is exactly the kind of line item a feasibility check surfaces before you commit.

03

Design fit matters

Small-scale residential properties sit in Development Permit Areas, so the city reviews how dwellings and landscaping fit the neighbourhood. Designing for those guidelines from day one is what unlocks the faster staff-level approval.

Strong SSMUH areas include Downtown PoCo, Riverwood, and Oxford Heights.

Port Coquitlam FAQ

Local questions, local answers

How many units can I build in Port Coquitlam?

Port Coquitlam permits 3 to 6 SSMUH units on properties in its RS residential zones (RS1 through RS4), with the 6-unit option tied to frequent transit proximity. Check your property's zoning on PoCoMAP.

Do I need a development permit in Port Coquitlam?

Yes. New duplexes, developments with 3 or more units, and accessory dwelling units require a SSMUH Development Permit before a building permit can be issued. Designs that comply with the zoning bylaw and OCP guidelines can be approved directly by staff under delegated authority, which speeds things up considerably.

What are the hidden costs in Port Coquitlam?

Two categories to budget early: development cost charges, which apply to SSMUH projects but not to new secondary suites or accessory buildings, and offsite servicing costs for connecting to city infrastructure. The city's own guide notes recent corner lots with lane access on two sides have seen more than $100,000 in additional offsite costs.

When did Port Coquitlam's SSMUH rules take effect?

Port Coquitlam brought its SSMUH framework into effect in 2024, permitting 3 to 6 units on RS-zoned properties, and pairs it with a development permit process that council has delegated to staff for compliant designs.
