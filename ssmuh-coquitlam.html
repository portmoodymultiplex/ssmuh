The Rules Here

What Coquitlam allows

Council adopted its SSMUH zoning bylaw changes on June 9, 2025, replacing the old single-family framework with small-scale residential zones. Lots larger than 280 square metres (about 3,000 square feet) permit up to 4 units; smaller lots permit up to 3.

One detail almost nobody communicates: Coquitlam currently has no bus stops that meet the provincial frequent-service criteria, so the 6-unit option does not apply anywhere in the city yet. If a consultant is promising you a sixplex in Coquitlam today, ask them to show you the qualifying transit stop.

Before You Plan

Three Coquitlam-specific checks

01

Confirm your zone on QtheMap

The city's mapping tool shows your property's current zoning and land use designation. This is the two-minute check that anchors everything else.

02

Fire flow paperwork is mandatory

Coquitlam requires a Small-Scale Residential Fire Flow Requirements Form with your building permit application, confirming enough water is available for firefighting. Checking available fire flow early avoids late surprises.

03

Older homes get screened first

If the existing house on your lot was built before 1945, the city asks you to contact Development Services before proceeding. Build that conversation into your timeline.

Also on the horizon: the city has signalled a review of townhouse, rowhouse, and intensive small-scale policies targeted for 2026, which could expand options further. Strong SSMUH neighbourhoods today include Town Centre, Burke Mountain, Westwood Plateau, and Eagle Ridge.

Coquitlam FAQ

Local questions, local answers

How many units can I build in Coquitlam?

Up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres (and up to 3 on smaller lots). Coquitlam currently has no bus stops meeting the provincial frequent-service criteria, so the 6-unit option does not apply anywhere in the city at this time.

Do I need to rezone in Coquitlam?

No. Council adopted small-scale residential zoning in June 2025, so qualifying lots already permit multi-unit housing. Confirm your property's zone and land use designation on the city's QtheMap tool before planning.

Are there special requirements in Coquitlam?

Two worth knowing early: a Small-Scale Residential Fire Flow Requirements Form must accompany your building permit application, and if the existing house was built before 1945 the city asks you to contact Development Services first.
