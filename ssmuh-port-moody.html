The Rules Here

What Port Moody allows

Most single-family lots in Port Moody permit up to 4 units as-of-right, and 515 lots near frequent transit qualify for up to 6. In total, city data identifies 3,709 lots eligible for 4-unit multiplexes. The city publishes an interactive SSMUH map where you can check a specific address.

Learned Firsthand

What the first application taught us

Working through Port Moody's process on his own property gave Dawar a view most consultants do not have. Three lessons carry over to almost every lot in the city:

01

Servicing decides more than zoning

Water and fire flow capacity vary block by block. Confirming what the mains near your lot can support belongs at the very start, because it can shape unit count, sprinkler requirements, and budget.

02

Access and grade shape the site plan

Port Moody has real topography. Lane access requirements and grade differentials on sloped lots affect where driveways and parking can go, which in turn affects the whole design.

03

Early alignment saves months

Understanding how the city reads its own standards before drawings are commissioned keeps the permit review on rails. Redesigning after submission is where timelines go to die.

Key neighbourhoods with strong SSMUH potential: Glenayre, Seaview, College Park, and Heritage Mountain.

Port Moody FAQ

Local questions, local answers

How many Port Moody lots qualify for SSMUH?

City of Port Moody data identifies 3,709 lots eligible for 4-unit multiplexes and 515 lots eligible for 6 units near frequent transit. The city publishes an interactive SSMUH map where you can look up a specific property, and a feasibility check confirms eligibility against the current bylaw.

Do I need to rezone in Port Moody?

No. Qualifying lots already permit small-scale multi-unit housing as-of-right. You need a building permit and a design that meets the city's setback, height, and parking standards, not a rezoning application or public hearing.

What trips up Port Moody multiplex projects?

Servicing is the biggest early check: water and fire flow capacity vary block by block and can shape what is buildable. Lane access and lot grade also affect driveway and site design on some streets. A feasibility review screens for these before you spend on drawings.
