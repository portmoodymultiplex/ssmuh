The Rules Changed

What SSMUH means for your property

Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing is provincial legislation (Bill 44, passed in 2023) that requires BC municipalities to allow multiple homes on residential lots that used to be single-family only.

01

No rezoning required

The political hurdle that used to take years is gone. If your lot qualifies, multi-unit housing is already permitted as-of-right. You need a building permit and a compliant design, not a public hearing.

02

Up to 4 homes on most lots, 6 near transit

Most single-family lots between 280 and 4,050 square metres qualify for up to four units. Lots within 400 metres of frequent transit may qualify for six.

03

Keep family close

Suites and laneway homes with separate entrances let adult kids, aging parents, and grandkids live on one lot with privacy and independence. This is the strongest tool for multigenerational living BC has ever had.

04

Income from land you already own

Rental suites can offset your mortgage. A stratified multiplex can be sold unit by unit. Either way, the value comes from property you worked decades to hold, without selling it.
