For Homeowners

What your property can become under the new rules.

Build a home for yourself, create suites for family, generate rental income, or all of the above. Here are the three paths Tri-Cities homeowners ask about most, with realistic numbers.

Cost and rent figures below are typical Metro Vancouver ranges for planning purposes, not quotes. Your feasibility check narrows them to your lot.

Shortcut: text Dawar directly at 604-808-3797 with your address. He answers his own phone, and he'll tell you straight what your lot allows. No form needed.

Path One

House + two suites

Keep your main home and add two legal suites. Nobody moves, and the suites carry a meaningful share of the mortgage.

Your Home

Keep the main house

You stay put. Phased construction is possible, so life continues while the suites take shape.

Suite One

1 to 2 bedrooms

Typical cost $80K to $160K. Typical rent $1,900 to $2,400 per month. The highest return on the smallest spend.

Suite Two

Double the income

A second suite at similar cost brings combined rental income to roughly $3,800 to $4,800 per month on the same lot.

Path Two

Fourplex

Four self-contained homes on one lot, each with its own entrance. The biggest project, and usually the strongest financial outcome.

The Build

Full redevelopment

Typical all-in cost $1.2M to $2.2M+ depending on size and finish. Design for stratification from day one so every exit stays open.

The Income

$8K to $11K gross monthly

Four units renting between $1,900 and $4,000 each depending on bedroom count. Live in one and the other three still work hard.

The Exit

Strata and sell, or hold

Sell units individually, keep the building as a rental asset, or mix both. Multiple exit strategies is the point.

Path Three

House + laneway + suite

Keep the main house, add a laneway home at the back and a suite inside. Flexibility without full redevelopment, and the best fit for multigenerational plans.

Laneway Home

2 to 3 bedrooms

Typical cost $250K to $450K, typical rent $2,400 to $3,100 per month. Also ideal for aging parents or an adult child living independently.

Secondary Suite

Inside the main home

Typical cost $80K to $160K. A qualifying suite for a senior or family member with a disability can return up to $7,500 through the federal MHRTC.

The Shape

Phased and livable

Laneway first, suite second, renovations as budget allows. Combined rental potential of roughly $4,300 to $5,500 per month if both units rent.

Why Homeowners Do This

Four outcomes, one lot

01

Downsize without leaving your street

Build a smaller, accessible home on your own lot and stay in the neighbourhood you know.

02

Keep adult kids close

Independent suites mean proximity without sharing walls. They build equity; you keep Sunday dinners.

03

Care for aging parents

A laneway home or ground-level suite keeps parents nearby with dignity and their own front door.

04

Build income and long-term value

Rental units pay down the mortgage while the property works as a multi-unit asset instead of a single home.
